Fleet Types

Fleet types we support

Semi Trucks from 5%, Box Trucks and Hotshot Trucks at 10% — plus a flat weekly option at $275 per truck per week.

Dispatch Fee: 5%

Semi Trucks

Dispatch support for eligible semi-truck operations, including:

  • Dry Van — general, non-temperature-controlled freight in enclosed trailers.
  • Reefer — refrigerated or temperature-controlled freight, subject to equipment capabilities.
  • Flatbed — open-deck freight that meets trailer specifications and securement capabilities.
  • Step Deck — freight requiring a step-deck trailer, subject to dimensions, weight, and loading requirements.
Dispatch Fee: 10%

Box Trucks

Support for box-truck operators seeking freight suited to their vehicle dimensions, payload capacity, operating area, and preferred routes — including load sourcing, rate discussions, booking coordination, shipment updates, and document organization.

Dispatch Fee: 10%

Hotshot Trucks

Support for hotshot operators seeking compatible freight based on trailer configuration, payload capacity, cargo dimensions, lane preferences, and operating requirements — including load sourcing, rate negotiation, booking, communication, and paperwork.

Service availability depends on equipment specifications, freight requirements, lanes, and the carrier's operating preferences. The applicable revenue basis and fee terms for each equipment type must be defined in the carrier agreement.

How We Work

A structured, carrier-approved workflow

From first conversation to completed-load records — the carrier approves every load before it is booked.

Step 1

Carrier Onboarding

We discuss the carrier's equipment, operating preferences, preferred lanes, requirements, and agreed service terms.

Step 2

Load Search

We identify potential freight opportunities that match the carrier's equipment and preferences.

Step 3

Rate Discussion

We communicate with brokers to discuss load details, rates, timing, and relevant conditions.

Step 4

Carrier Approval

The carrier reviews and approves the load before it is booked.

Step 5

Booking and Documentation

We coordinate the booking process and organize the relevant load documentation.

Step 6

Pickup and Shipment Coordination

We help coordinate pickup details and communicate relevant shipment updates.

Step 7

Delivery and POD Collection

We follow up on delivery status and help organize proof-of-delivery documentation.

Step 8

Records and Ongoing Planning

We maintain dispatch-related records according to the agreed workflow and continue looking for suitable opportunities.
